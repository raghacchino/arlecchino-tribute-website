Arlecchino

Arlecchino, also known by her codename "The Knave," is a playable Pyro polearm character in Genshin Impact. She is the Fourth of the Eleven Fatui Harbingers and the current head of the House of the Hearth. She is addressed as "Father" by members of the House, who she calls her "children."

Arlecchino's childhood name was Peruere. Upon claiming the title of The Knave, she forsook her name and donned a new one befiting her position.

Peruere was an orphan at the House of the Hearth which at the time was ruled by Crucabena, the former Knave of the Fatui and eventually became known to the House's children as Mother.
